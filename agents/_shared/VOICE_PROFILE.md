# Voice profile (template)

Replace this file with your own voice and brand rules. Agents that write anything a person will read (emails, documents, comments) read it before writing.

Suggested contents:
- **Tone:** who the reader is, how formal, how warm.
- **Words to use and words to avoid.**
- **Punctuation habits** (for example: no em-dashes in client-facing copy).
- **Sign-off and signature block.**
- **Examples:** two or three short samples of writing that sounds like you.

Until you fill it in, agents default to plain, direct English with short sentences.
